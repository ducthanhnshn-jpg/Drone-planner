Time-optimal trajectory planning for a drone in a field of obstacles

2D model · Python 3 with NumPy · limits of a DJI Inspire 3 class drone

Abstract

This project plans the fastest flight of a drone between two points on a 100 × 100 m field scattered with circular obstacles. Each path is assembled from straight segments, clothoids and circular arcs, and every point on it is given a speed. Speed, acceleration and jerk stay within the limits of a DJI Inspire 3 class drone, and the drone's body, a disc of radius 2 m, never touches an obstacle.

The method uses only high-school geometry and kinematics: circles, tangent lines, vectors and a Taylor series. On a benchmark of 290 maps the planner found a path on 289. The planned flight time is a median of 0.014 s above a theoretical lower bound, and no limit is ever exceeded.

Objectives

  • Find the path and speed profile with the least flight time from A to B.

  • Keep the drone's body, a disc of radius 2 m, clear of every obstacle.
  • Build every step from formulas that can be checked by hand.

    2 · Problem

    Problem statement

    A drone starts at rest at point A and must come to rest at point B. The field measures 100 × 100 m and contains 15 circular obstacles with radii between 2 and 6 m, placed at random. At least one obstacle always blocks the straight line from A to B.

    The drone is modelled as a disc of radius 2 m. Its centre must therefore stay outside a safety circle of radius r = R + 2 around each obstacle of radius R. The task is to choose a path and a speed along it that minimise the flight time T.

    minimise T   subject to   v ≤ 26.1 m/s,   |a| ≤ 45.4 m/s²,   |j| ≤ 215 m/s³,   distance to each obstacle ≥ R + 2 m

    Why the problem is difficult

    Turns are wider than obstacles

    At 26.1 m/s the tightest turn has radius v²/a ≈ 15 m, larger than any obstacle. A fast path cannot hug a small obstacle.

    Acceleration cannot jump

    With jerk limited to 215 m/s³, sideways acceleration takes time to build up. A straight line cannot be joined directly to a circle.

    The number of routes grows quickly

    Each obstacle can be passed on the left, on the right or not at all, so the number of candidate routes grows rapidly with the number of obstacles.

    5 · Method

    Method

    The planner works in seven steps. Each step is a short formula from geometry or kinematics, so every number it produces can be checked by hand. No grid search or graph algorithm is used.

    1. 5.1

      Top speed everywhere

      If the drone flies at top speed everywhere except near A and B, the flight time depends only on the path length L. Minimising time then reduces to finding the shortest path the drone can actually fly.

      T = L/v + v/a + a/j  ·  reaching 26.1 m/s takes 0.786 s and 10.26 m
    2. 5.2

      Turning radius

      At speed v, a turn of radius ρ needs a centripetal acceleration v²/ρ. Requiring v²/ρ ≤ a gives the smallest radius. The turning circle is then moved away from the obstacle along the bisector n of the turn until it touches the safety circle at one point.

      r = R + 2  ·  ρ = max(r, v²/a) = 15.0 m for r ≤ 15 m  ·  O = C + (ρ − r)·n
    3. 5.3

      Clothoid transitions

      A straight line cannot be joined directly to a circle: the curvature would jump, and the sideways acceleration with it. With jerk limited, the curvature has to change gradually. Letting it grow in proportion to distance keeps the jerk exactly at its limit (section 6.3). The curve with this property is the clothoid, and its coordinates follow from a Taylor series of sin and cos.

      jq = v³/ρ² = 79  ·  jn = √(j² − jq²) = 200  ·  Ls = v³/(ρjn) = 5.93 m  ·  τ = Ls/(2ρ) = 11.3°
      κ(s) = s/A²  ·  x = s − s⁵/(40A⁴) + s⁹/(3456A⁸)  ·  y = s³/(6A²) − s⁷/(336A⁶) + s¹¹/(42240A¹⁰)
    4. 5.4

      Tangent lines

      The straight segments are common tangents to consecutive turning circles. A signed-distance argument gives the direction θ of each tangent in one formula. Each clothoid starts a distance k before the tangent point and ends k after it.

      θ = φ − arcsin(c/|D|),  c = σ₂r₂ − σ₁r₁  ·  T₁ = E₁ − ku₁,  T₆ = E₂ + ku₂,  k = 2.96 m
    5. 5.5

      Choosing the obstacles to pass

      The search starts from the straight line A–B. The first obstacle it crosses is tried on the left and on the right, and the path is rebuilt and checked again. A second, depth-first search tries other orders and discards any branch whose length can no longer beat the best path found.

      keep the valid path with the smallest L  ·  at most 6 turns  ·  discard a branch if Lso far + |P − B| ≥ Lbest
    6. 5.6

      Touching turns

      In a tight S-bend two consecutive turns can overlap at full speed. A lower speed shrinks both turning circles, so the planner bisects the speed interval 18 times to find the highest speed vs at which the turns touch at exactly one point X. It then computes where braking must begin. This case is used only when it gives a shorter flight time than the best route found at full speed.

      ρ = vs²/a  ·  braking distance d = (v₁ + vs)t/2,  t = Δv/a + a/j (section 6.4)
    7. 5.7

      Speed profile

      On straight segments the drone accelerates with the jerk sequence +j, 0, −j and brakes with −j, 0, +j, so the acceleration ramps up and down instead of jumping. Inside turns the speed is constant, because the whole acceleration budget is already used sideways.

      jerk +j, 0, −j  |  cruise  |  −j, 0, +j  ·  a(t) = a₀ + jt,  v(t) = v₀ + a₀t + jt²/2
    6 · Physics

    Physics of the flight

    The planner treats the drone as a point moving along a path. Its motion is described by the speed v, the acceleration a and the jerk j, which is the rate of change of acceleration. This section derives the formulas of section 5 from these definitions and shows where each limit is reached.

    6.1 Two parts of the acceleration

    On a curved path the acceleration has two perpendicular parts. The tangential part at changes the speed. The normal part an points to the centre of the turn and changes the direction of motion; for a turn of radius ρ it equals v²/ρ, or v²κ with the curvature κ = 1/ρ. The limit of 45.4 m/s² applies to the total.

    at = dv/dt  ·  an = v²/ρ = v²κ
    |a| = √(at² + an²) ≤ 45.4 m/s²

    6.2 Minimum turning radius

    In a turn flown at constant speed, at = 0 and the whole acceleration points sideways. The condition v²/ρ ≤ a gives the smallest radius. Because the radius grows with the square of the speed, halving the speed makes the smallest turn four times tighter. This is why the planner slows down when two turns would overlap.

    v²/ρ ≤ a  ⇒  ρ ≥ v²/a = 26.1²/45.4 = 15.0 m

    6.3 Jerk in a turn and the length of the clothoid

    At constant speed the normal acceleration an = v²κ changes for two reasons. Its direction turns with the drone at the angular rate ω = vκ, which gives a jerk component jT along the path. Its size changes when the curvature changes, which gives a component jN towards the centre.

    jT = anω = v³κ²  ·  jN = d(v²κ)/dt = v³ dκ/ds  ·  |j| = √(jT² + jN²) ≤ 215 m/s³

    On the arc the curvature is constant, so jN = 0 and only jT = v³/ρ² = 79 m/s³ remains. In the clothoid the curvature grows at the constant rate dκ/ds = 1/A², so jN = v³/A² stays fixed while jT grows. The total jerk is therefore largest at the end of the clothoid, where κ = 1/ρ and jT = 79 m/s³. Choosing jN = √(j² − jT²) = 200 m/s³ makes the total exactly 215 m/s³ there and gives the clothoid length used in step 5.3:

    v³/A² = 200  ⇒  A² = v³/200
    κ(Ls) = Ls/A² = 1/ρ  ⇒  Ls = A²/ρ = v³/(200ρ) = 5.93 m

    6.4 Speeding up and braking

    On a straight segment only at acts. The jerk is held at +j until the acceleration reaches a, the acceleration is then held at a, and finally a jerk of −j brings it back to zero exactly when the speed reaches v. The profile is symmetric, so the average speed is v/2.

    tacc = v/a + a/j = 0.575 + 0.211 = 0.786 s  ·  sacc = vtacc/2 = 10.26 m

    Accelerating at A and braking at B each take tacc and cover sacc; the rest of the path is flown at v. This gives the flight-time formula of step 5.1:

    T = 2tacc + (L − 2sacc)/v = L/v + tacc = L/v + v/a + a/j

    A change between two speeds v₁ and v₂ follows the same pattern. With Δv = |v₂ − v₁|, it takes t = Δv/a + a/j when Δv ≥ a²/j = 9.59 m/s and t = 2√(Δv/j) otherwise, and in both cases covers the distance d = (v₁ + v₂)t/2.

    6.5 What the acceleration limit requires

    A drone accelerates sideways by tilting its thrust. To hold altitude, the vertical part of the thrust must balance gravity while the horizontal part produces a. At the limit this needs a bank angle of 77.8° and a thrust of 4.7 times the weight. Section 7.2 discusses whether a real drone can do this.

    tan β = a/g = 45.4/9.81  ⇒  β = 77.8°
    thrust/m = √(g² + a²) = 46.4 m/s² = 4.7g

    7.1 Turns are always flown at top speed

    In this simulator run, B was placed by hand in a pocket only 3.7 to 3.9 m from three safety circles. The planner returned a 203 m loop round the far corner of the field, about twice the length of the direct routes on either side of the central cluster.

    Why it happens

    1. At 26.1 m/s the turning radius is at least 15 m. Around an obstacle next to B, the turning circle is pushed about 10 m outwards and B ends up inside it, so no tangent line can reach B. The last turn also cannot end 10.26 m before B, as braking requires. Every route flown at full speed fails, and the main search finds no path at all.
    2. The planner lowers the speed in one situation only: when two consecutive turns overlap. It never slows down for a single turn. The two faster routes fail because no tangent reaches B, not because turns overlap, so they are never retried at a lower speed.
    3. Among the routes rejected for overlapping turns, the fastest one that can be repaired is the loop round the far corner. Slowing its last two turns to 21.2 m/s, where they just touch, shrinks the final turn to a radius of 9.9 m, small enough to reach B from the north. This loop is the only path the planner returns.

    The test cases did not reveal this, because the random map generator keeps every obstacle at least 12 m from A and B; under that rule the planner solved 400 of 400 maps. Without the rule, the planner finds no path on 2.8% of 400 random maps, and on 16% of another 118 maps a lower speed in single turns would save time, by up to 0.26 s.

    The two faster routes come from a separate search that gives each turn its own speed; the planner itself does not produce them. Both were checked for collisions and for the v, a and j limits. The map was rebuilt from a screenshot of the simulator, and the planner reproduces the original result (203 m, 9.08 s).

    7.2 Realism of the model

    • The drone is a point moving in a plane. Thrust, attitude, drag, wind and battery are not modelled.
    • The 45.4 m/s² limit is the same in every direction and at every speed. In level flight it needs a bank angle of 77.8° and a thrust of 4.7 times the weight.
    • There is no safety margin: the path touches the safety circles, so any tracking error means contact between the drone and an obstacle.
    • The trajectory is computed once and flown without feedback or re-planning.

    7.3 Snap

    Jerk changes instantly at T1, T3, T4 and T6 and at every switch of the speed profile; at the start of a clothoid it jumps from 0 to 200 m/s³ (the vertical edges in the |j| charts). Snap, the rate of change of jerk, is therefore unbounded at these instants. For a quadrotor, snap corresponds to the angular acceleration of the body, which the motors can only produce gradually, so a real drone lags behind the path there. Minimum-snap planners avoid this by keeping jerk continuous.

    s = dj/dt → ∞ at each jump of j
    minimum snap: minimise ∫ s² dt

    The common tangent of two circles from one signed-distance formula, and the bisector n of a turn.

    def common_tangent(O1, r1, s1, O2, r2, s2):
    D = O2 - O1
    dD = math.hypot(D[0], D[1])
    c = s2 * r2 - s1 * r1
    if dD < 1e-9 or abs(c) > dD:
        return None
    th = math.atan2(D[1], D[0]) - math.asin(c / dD)
    u = np.array([math.cos(th), math.sin(th)])
    return O1 - s1 * r1 * left(u), O2 - s2 * r2 * left(u), u


def bisector(P, C, R, s, Q):
    t1 = common_tangent(P, 0.0, 1, C, R, s)
    t2 = common_tangent(C, R, s, Q, 0.0, 1)
    if t1 is None or t2 is None:
        return None
    w = t2[2] - t1[2]
    nw = np.linalg.norm(w)
    if nw < 1e-9:
        return s * left(t1[2])
    return w / nw

    def build_slowed(A, B, seq, zones, T_best=math.inf, iters=BISECT_ITERS):
    v_turn = [V] * len(seq)
    done = []
    for _ in range(len(seq)):
        err = {}
        traj = build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, v_turn if done else None, err=err)
        if traj is not None:
            for (m1, m2) in done:
                t1, t2 = traj["turns"][m1], traj["turns"][m2]
                traj["slowed_pairs"].append(dict(m1=m1, m2=m2, v_s=v_turn[m1], X=(t1["T6"] + t2["T1"]) / 2,
                                                 s_X=(t1["s_end"] + t2["s_start"]) / 2, gap=t2["straight_before"],
                                                 rho1=t1["rho"], rho2=t2["rho"]))
            return traj if done else None
        pair = err.get("pair")
        if pair is None or pair in done:
            return None
        m1, m2 = pair

        def fits(v):
            vv = list(v_turn); vv[m1] = vv[m2] = v
            e = {}
            q = build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, vv, err=e)
            return (q is not None or (e.get("pair") is not None and e["pair"] != pair)), q

        ok, q_min = fits(V_MIN_TURN)
        if not ok:
            return None
        if q_min is not None and flight_time(q_min["L"]) >= T_best:
            return None
        lo, hi = V_MIN_TURN, min(v_turn[m1], v_turn[m2])
        for _ in range(iters):
            mid = (lo + hi) / 2
            if fits(mid)[0]:

            else:

    The fastest change from speed v1 to v2 under the acceleration and jerk limits.

    def speed_change(v1, v2):
    dv = v2 - v1
    sg = 1.0 if dv >= 0 else -1.0
    dv = abs(dv)
    if dv < 1e-12:
        return [], 0.0, 0.0
    if dv >= AMAX ** 2 / JMAX:
        tj = AMAX / JMAX
        phases = [(tj, sg * JMAX), (dv / AMAX - tj, 0.0), (tj, -sg * JMAX)]
    else:
        tj = math.sqrt(dv / JMAX)
        phases = [(tj, sg * JMAX), (tj, -sg * JMAX)]
    t = sum(p[0] for p in phases)
    return phases, t, (v1 + v2) / 2 * t

def fresnel(s, A2):
    x = s - s ** 5 / (40 * A2 ** 2) + s ** 9 / (3456 * A2 ** 4)
    y = s ** 3 / (6 * A2) - s ** 7 / (336 * A2 ** 3) + s ** 11 / (42240 * A2 ** 5)
    return x, y

def small_turn(Delta, c):
    Lh = math.sqrt(Delta * c["rho"] * c["Ls"])
    xh, yh = fresnel(Lh, c["A2"])
    h = Delta / 2
    rho_t = yh / (1 - math.cos(h))
    XV = xh + yh * math.tan(h)
    k = XV - rho_t * math.tan(h)
    return dict(Lh=Lh, xh=xh, yh=yh, rho_t=rho_t, XV=XV, k=k)

def random_map(rng):
    while True:
        A = rng.uniform(EDGE_MARGIN, MAP_SIZE - EDGE_MARGIN, 2)
        B = rng.uniform(EDGE_MARGIN, MAP_SIZE - EDGE_MARGIN, 2)
        if np.linalg.norm(B - A) >= MIN_AB_DIST:
            break
    obstacles, tries = [], 0
    while len(obstacles) < N_OBSTACLES and tries < 3000:
        tries += 1
        C = rng.uniform(0, MAP_SIZE, 2)
        R = rng.uniform(R_OBS_MIN, R_OBS_MAX)
        if min(np.linalg.norm(C - A), np.linalg.norm(C - B)) < R + R_DRONE + AB_CLEARANCE:
            continue
        obstacles.append((C, R))
    return (A, B, obstacles) if len(obstacles) == N_OBSTACLES else None


def safety_zones(obstacles):
    return [(C, R + R_DRONE) for C, R in obstacles]


def point_segment_distance(P, Q, C):
    d = Q - P
    t = np.clip(np.dot(C - P, d) / max(np.dot(d, d), 1e-12), 0, 1)
    return float(np.linalg.norm(P + t * d - C))


def straight_line_blocked(A, B, zones):
    return any(point_segment_distance(A, B, C) < R for C, R in zones)


def heading(u):
    return math.atan2(u[1], u[0])


def left(u):
    return np.array([-u[1], u[0]])


def common_tangent(O1, r1, s1, O2, r2, s2):
    D = O2 - O1
    dD = math.hypot(D[0], D[1])
    c = s2 * r2 - s1 * r1
    if dD < 1e-9 or abs(c) > dD:
        return None
    th = math.atan2(D[1], D[0]) - math.asin(c / dD)
    u = np.array([math.cos(th), math.sin(th)])
    return O1 - s1 * r1 * left(u), O2 - s2 * r2 * left(u), u


def bisector(P, C, R, s, Q):
    t1 = common_tangent(P, 0.0, 1, C, R, s)
    t2 = common_tangent(C, R, s, Q, 0.0, 1)
    if t1 is None or t2 is None:
        return None
    w = t2[2] - t1[2]
    nw = np.linalg.norm(w)
    if nw < 1e-9:
        return s * left(t1[2])
    return w / nw


def _fail(err, name, pair=None):
    if err is not None:
        err["error"], err["pair"] = name, pair
    return None


def build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, v_turn=None, err=None, step_straight=0.25, step_curve=0.05):
    m_ = len(seq)
    slowed = v_turn is not None
    v_turn = list(v_turn) if slowed else [V] * m_
    cs = [turn_constants(max(zones[i][1], v_turn[m] ** 2 / AMAX), v_turn[m]) for m, (i, _) in enumerate(seq)]
    if any(c is None for c in cs):
        return _fail(err, "jerk")
    centers = [A] + [zones[i][0] for i, _ in seq] + [B]
    n = []
    for m, (i, s) in enumerate(seq):
        nm = bisector(centers[m], zones[i][0], zones[i][1], s, centers[m + 2])
        if nm is None:
            return _fail(err, "bisector")
        n.append(nm)
    r_tan = [c["rho"] + c["p"] for c in cs]
    r_apex = [c["rho"] for c in cs]
    st = [None] * m_
    for rnd in range(2):
        O = [zones[i][0] + (r_apex[m] - zones[i][1]) * n[m] for m, (i, s) in enumerate(seq)]
        nodes = [(A, 0.0, 1)] + [(O[m], r_tan[m], s) for m, (i, s) in enumerate(seq)] + [(B, 0.0, 1)]
        tt = []
        for g, (a, b) in enumerate(zip(nodes[:-1], nodes[1:])):
            t = common_tangent(*a, *b)
            if t is None:
                return _fail(err, "tangent", (g - 1, g) if 1 <= g <= m_ - 1 else None)
            tt.append(t)
        Delta = []
        for m, (i, s) in enumerate(seq):
            De = (s * (heading(tt[m + 1][2]) - heading(tt[m][2]))) % (2 * math.pi)
            if De > math.pi:
                return _fail(err, "turn > 180°")
            Delta.append(De)
        if rnd == 1:
            break
        changed = False
        for m in range(m_):
            if Delta[m] < 2 * cs[m]["tau"]:
                st[m] = small_turn(max(Delta[m], 1e-4), cs[m])
                r_tan[m] = r_apex[m] = st[m]["rho_t"]
                changed = True
        if not changed:
            break
    XY, K, kind = [A.reshape(1, 2)], [np.zeros(1)], [np.zeros(1, int)]
    geom_marks, turns, cur, L = [], [], A, 0.0
    for m, (i, s) in enumerate(seq):
        c = cs[m]
        rho, Ls, tau, A2 = c["rho"], c["Ls"], c["tau"], c["A2"]
        u1, u2 = tt[m][2], tt[m + 1][2]
        E1, E2 = tt[m][1], tt[m + 1][0]
        De = Delta[m]
        has_arc = De >= 2 * tau
        if has_arc:
            if st[m] is not None and not slowed:
                return _fail(err, "turn type")
            Lh = Ls
            if st[m] is None:
                T1 = E1 - c["k"] * u1
                T6 = E2 + c["k"] * u2
                Oc = O[m]
            else:
                lam = np.dot(E2 - E1, left(u2)) / np.dot(u1, left(u2))
                Vd = E1 + lam * u1
                XV = c["k"] + (rho + c["p"]) * math.tan(De / 2)
                T1, T6 = Vd - XV * u1, Vd + XV * u2
                E1, E2 = T1 + c["k"] * u1, T6 - c["k"] * u2
                Oc = E1 + s * (rho + c["p"]) * left(u1)
            b1, b2 = heading(E1 - Oc), heading(E2 - Oc)
            T3 = Oc + rho * np.array([math.cos(b1 + s * tau), math.sin(b1 + s * tau)])
            T4 = Oc + rho * np.array([math.cos(b2 - s * tau), math.sin(b2 - s * tau)])
        else:
            if De < 1e-6:
                return _fail(err, "no turn")
            r = small_turn(De, c)
            Lh = r["Lh"]
            lam = np.dot(E2 - E1, left(u2)) / np.dot(u1, left(u2))
            Vd = E1 + lam * u1
            T1 = Vd - r["XV"] * u1
            T6 = Vd + r["XV"] * u2
            Oc = None
        l_straight = float(np.dot(T1 - cur, u1))
        if l_straight < -1e-6:
            return _fail(err, "overlap", (m - 1, m) if m >= 1 else None)
        nt = max(math.ceil(l_straight / step_straight) + 1, 2)
        XY.append(np.linspace(cur, T1, nt)[1:]); K.append(np.zeros(nt - 1)); kind.append(np.zeros(nt - 1, int))
        sv = np.linspace(0, Lh, max(math.ceil(Lh / step_curve) + 1, 3))
        x, y = fresnel(sv, A2)
        P_in = T1 + np.outer(x, u1) + s * np.outer(y, left(u1))
        if has_arc:
            La = rho * (De - 2 * tau)
            bb = b1 + s * tau + s * np.linspace(0, De - 2 * tau, max(math.ceil(La / step_curve) + 1, 2))
            P_arc = Oc + rho * np.column_stack([np.cos(bb), np.sin(bb)])
        else:
            La, P_arc = 0.0, np.empty((0, 2))
        x, y = fresnel(sv[::-1], A2)
        P_out = T6 - np.outer(x, u2) + s * np.outer(y, left(u2))
        nc = max(len(P_arc) - 1, 0)
        XY.append(np.vstack([P_in[1:], P_arc[1:], P_out[1:]]))
        K.append(s * np.r_[sv[1:] / A2, np.full(nc, 1 / rho), sv[::-1][1:] / A2])
        kind.append(np.r_[np.ones(len(sv) - 1, int), np.full(nc, 2), np.ones(len(sv) - 1, int)])
        Lc = 2 * Lh + La
        s0 = L + l_straight
        geom_marks.append((s0, "T1", f"clothoid start (obstacle {i})"))
        if has_arc:
            geom_marks.append((s0 + Lh, "T3", f"arc start, rho={rho:.1f} m"))
            geom_marks.append((s0 + Lc - Lh, "T4", "arc end"))
        else:
            geom_marks.append((s0 + Lc / 2, "M", f"max curvature point (obstacle {i})"))
        geom_marks.append((s0 + Lc, "T6", "clothoid end"))
        turns.append(dict(obstacle=i, side=s, R=zones[i][1], rho=rho, has_arc=has_arc, Delta=De, O=Oc, n=n[m],
                          E1=E1, E2=E2, T1=T1, T6=T6, T3=T3 if has_arc else None, T4=T4 if has_arc else None,
                          r_tangent=r_tan[m], O_draw=Oc if has_arc else O[m], s_start=s0, s_end=s0 + Lc,
                          v=v_turn[m], straight_before=max(l_straight, 0.0)))
        L += l_straight + Lc
        cur = T6
    l_last = float(np.linalg.norm(B - cur))
    nt = max(math.ceil(l_last / step_straight) + 1, 2)
    XY.append(np.linspace(cur, B, nt)[1:]); K.append(np.zeros(nt - 1)); kind.append(np.zeros(nt - 1, int))
    L += l_last
    XY, K, kind = np.vstack(XY), np.concatenate(K), np.concatenate(kind)
    S = np.concatenate([[0], np.cumsum(np.hypot(*np.diff(XY, axis=0).T))])
    S *= L / S[-1]
    return dict(seq=list(seq), XY=XY, K=K, kind=kind, S=S, L=L, geom_marks=geom_marks, turns=turns,
                last_straight=l_last, slowed_pairs=[])


def clearance_matrix(XY, Cs, Rs):
    return np.hypot(XY[:, None, 0] - Cs[None, :, 0], XY[:, None, 1] - Cs[None, :, 1]) - Rs[None, :]


def is_valid(traj, cl):
    if (cl < -TOL).any() or traj["XY"].min() < 0 or traj["XY"].max() > MAP_SIZE:
        return False
    t = traj["turns"]
    return not t or (t[0]["s_start"] >= S_ACC and t[-1]["s_end"] <= traj["L"] - S_ACC)


def find_trajectory(A, B, zones, overlapping=None):
    Cs = np.array([C for C, _ in zones])
    Rs = np.array([R for _, R in zones])
    best = [None]
    tried = set()

    def record(traj, cl):
        if is_valid(traj, cl) and (best[0] is None or traj["L"] < best[0]["L"]):
            traj["clearance"] = float(cl.min())
            best[0] = traj

    def insert(seq):
        if tuple(seq) in tried:
            return "tried"
        tried.add(tuple(seq))
        err = {}
        traj = build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, err=err)
        if traj is None:
            if overlapping is not None and err.get("pair") is not None:
                overlapping.append(list(seq))
            return "failed"
        if best[0] is not None and traj["L"] >= best[0]["L"]:
            return
        cl = clearance_matrix(traj["XY"], Cs, Rs)
        hit = cl < -TOL
        if not hit.any():
            record(traj, cl)
            return
        if len(seq) >= MAX_TURNS:
            return
        k = int(np.argmax(hit.any(axis=1)))
        j = int(np.argmax(hit[k]))
        if any(j == i for i, _ in seq):
            return
        pos = sum(1 for t in traj["turns"] if (t["s_start"] + t["s_end"]) / 2 < traj["S"][k])
        for s in (1, -1):
            if insert(seq[:pos] + [(j, s)] + seq[pos:]) == "failed":
                for q in (pos - 1, pos):
                    if 0 <= q < len(seq):
                        insert(seq[:q] + [(j, s)] + seq[q + 1:])

    insert([])

    def extend(seq):
        best_L = best[0]["L"] if best[0] is not None else math.inf
        err = {}
        traj = build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, err=err)
        used = {i for i, _ in seq}
        if traj is None:
            if overlapping is not None and err.get("pair") is not None:
                overlapping.append(list(seq))
            if len(seq) >= 3:
                return
            P = zones[seq[-1][0]][0] if seq else A
            for j in range(len(zones)):
                if j not in used and np.linalg.norm(Cs[j] - P) + np.linalg.norm(B - Cs[j]) - 2 * Rs[j] < best_L:
                    for s in (1, -1):
                        extend(seq + [(j, s)])
            return
        cl = clearance_matrix(traj["XY"], Cs, Rs)
        s_fixed = traj["turns"][-2]["s_start"] if len(seq) >= 2 else 0.0
        if (cl[traj["S"] < s_fixed] < -TOL).any():
            return
        P = traj["XY"][np.searchsorted(traj["S"], s_fixed)]
        if s_fixed + np.linalg.norm(B - P) >= best_L:
            return
        record(traj, cl)
        if len(seq) >= MAX_TURNS:
            return
        s_last = traj["turns"][-1]["s_start"] if seq else 0.0
        P = traj["XY"][np.searchsorted(traj["S"], s_last)]
        for j in range(len(zones)):
            if j in used:
                continue
            lb = s_last + max(np.linalg.norm(Cs[j] - P) - Rs[j], 0) + max(np.linalg.norm(B - Cs[j]) - Rs[j], 0)
            if lb >= (best[0]["L"] if best[0] is not None else math.inf):
                continue
            for s in (1, -1):
                extend(seq + [(j, s)])

    extend([])
    return best[0]


def build_slowed(A, B, seq, zones, T_best=math.inf, iters=BISECT_ITERS):
    v_turn = [V] * len(seq)
    done = []
    for _ in range(len(seq)):
        err = {}
        traj = build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, v_turn if done else None, err=err)
        if traj is not None:
            for (m1, m2) in done:
                t1, t2 = traj["turns"][m1], traj["turns"][m2]
                traj["slowed_pairs"].append(dict(m1=m1, m2=m2, v_s=v_turn[m1], X=(t1["T6"] + t2["T1"]) / 2,
                                                 s_X=(t1["s_end"] + t2["s_start"]) / 2, gap=t2["straight_before"],
                                                 rho1=t1["rho"], rho2=t2["rho"]))
            return traj if done else None
        pair = err.get("pair")
        if pair is None or pair in done:
            return None
        m1, m2 = pair

        def fits(v):
            vv = list(v_turn); vv[m1] = vv[m2] = v
            e = {}
            q = build_trajectory(A, B, seq, zones, vv, err=e)
            return (q is not None or (e.get("pair") is not None and e["pair"] != pair)), q

        ok, q_min = fits(V_MIN_TURN)
        if not ok:
            return None
        if q_min is not None and flight_time(q_min["L"]) >= T_best:
            return None
        lo, hi = V_MIN_TURN, min(v_turn[m1], v_turn[m2])
        for _ in range(iters):
            mid = (lo + hi) / 2
            if fits(mid)[0]:
                lo = mid
            else:
                hi = mid
        v_turn[m1] = v_turn[m2] = lo
        done.append(pair)
    return None


def speed_change(v1, v2):
    dv = v2 - v1
    sg = 1.0 if dv >= 0 else -1.0
    dv = abs(dv)
    if dv < 1e-12:
        return [], 0.0, 0.0
    if dv >= AMAX ** 2 / JMAX:
        tj = AMAX / JMAX
        phases = [(tj, sg * JMAX), (dv / AMAX - tj, 0.0), (tj, -sg * JMAX)]
    else:
        tj = math.sqrt(dv / JMAX)
        phases = [(tj, sg * JMAX), (tj, -sg * JMAX)]
    t = sum(p[0] for p in phases)
    return phases, t, (v1 + v2) / 2 * t


def speed_plan(traj):
    turns = traj["turns"]
    n = len(turns)
    straights = [t["straight_before"] for t in turns] + [traj["last_straight"]]
    w = [t["v"] for t in turns]
    phases, marks, t_, s_, v_in = [], [], 0.0, 0.0, 0.0
    for g in range(n + 1):
        v_out = w[g] if g < n else 0.0
        l = straights[g]
        if speed_change(v_in, v_out)[2] > l + 1e-6:
            return None
        total = lambda vp: speed_change(v_in, vp)[2] + speed_change(vp, v_out)[2]
        if total(V) <= l:
            vp = V
        else:
            lo, hi = max(v_in, v_out), V
            for _ in range(50):
                mid = (lo + hi) / 2
                lo, hi = (mid, hi) if total(mid) <= l else (lo, mid)
            vp = lo
        p1, t1, d1 = speed_change(v_in, vp)
        p2, t2, d2 = speed_change(vp, v_out)
        cruise = max(l - d1 - d2, 0.0)
        t_cruise = cruise / vp if cruise > 1e-12 else 0.0
        phases += p1
        if vp - v_in > 0.05:
            marks.append((t_ + t1, s_ + d1, "P1" if g == 0 else "EA", f"acceleration done, v = {vp:.2f} m/s"))
        if t_cruise > 0:
            phases.append((t_cruise, 0.0))
        if vp - v_out > 0.05:
            marks.append((t_ + t1 + t_cruise, s_ + d1 + cruise, "P2" if g == n else "SD",
                          "final braking starts" if g == n else f"deceleration to {v_out:.2f} m/s starts"))
        phases += p2
        t_ += t1 + t_cruise + t2
        s_ += l
        if g < n:
            lc = turns[g]["s_end"] - turns[g]["s_start"]
            phases.append((lc / v_out, 0.0))
            t_ += lc / v_out
            s_ += lc
        v_in = v_out
    return dict(phases=phases, T=t_, marks=marks)


def sample_phases(phases, L, dt=0.002):
    T = sum(d for d, _ in phases)
    t = np.linspace(0, T, int(math.ceil(T / dt)) + 1)
    s_, v_, a_, j_ = (np.zeros_like(t) for _ in range(4))
    t0 = s0 = v0 = a0 = 0.0
    for d, jj in phases:
        if d <= 0:
            continue
        m = (t >= t0 - 1e-12) & (t <= t0 + d + 1e-9)
        ta = t[m] - t0
        a_[m] = a0 + jj * ta
        v_[m] = v0 + a0 * ta + jj * ta ** 2 / 2
        s_[m] = s0 + v0 * ta + a0 * ta ** 2 / 2 + jj * ta ** 3 / 6
        j_[m] = jj
        s0 += v0 * d + a0 * d ** 2 / 2 + jj * d ** 3 / 6
        v0 += a0 * d + jj * d ** 2 / 2
        a0 += jj * d
        t0 += d
    return dict(t=t, s=np.clip(s_, 0, L), v=np.maximum(v_, 0), a=a_, jt=j_, T=T, s_end=s0, v_end=v0)


def check_touching_turns(A, B, zones, traj_normal, overlapping):
    Cs = np.array([C for C, _ in zones])
    Rs = np.array([R for _, R in zones])
    T_best = flight_time(traj_normal["L"]) if traj_normal is not None else math.inf
    best, seen = None, set()
    for seq in overlapping:
        if tuple(seq) in seen:
            continue
        seen.add(tuple(seq))
        q = build_slowed(A, B, seq, zones, T_best, iters=8)
        if q is None:
            continue
        cl = clearance_matrix(q["XY"], Cs, Rs)
        if (cl < -TOL).any() or q["XY"].min() < 0 or q["XY"].max() > MAP_SIZE:
            continue
        plan = speed_plan(q)
        if plan is not None and plan["T"] < T_best:
            best, T_best = seq, plan["T"]
    if best is None:
        return traj_normal
    q = build_slowed(A, B, best, zones)
    if q is None:
        return traj_normal
    cl = clearance_matrix(q["XY"], Cs, Rs)
    plan = speed_plan(q)
    if plan is None or (cl < -TOL).any() or q["XY"].min() < 0 or q["XY"].max() > MAP_SIZE:
        return traj_normal
    if traj_normal is not None and plan["T"] >= flight_time(traj_normal["L"]):
        return traj_normal
    q["T"], q["plan"], q["clearance"] = plan["T"], plan, float(cl.min())
    return q


def flight_time(L):
    return L / V + V / AMAX + AMAX / JMAX


def s_curve_profile(L, dt=0.002):
    a, j, v = AMAX, JMAX, V
    if L >= v * (v / a + a / j):
        tj, tc = a / j, v / a - a / j
        tcr = (L - v * (v / a + a / j)) / v
    elif L >= 2 * a ** 3 / j ** 2:
        vp = a / 2 * (-a / j + math.sqrt(a ** 2 / j ** 2 + 4 * L / a))
        tj, tc, tcr = a / j, vp / a - a / j, 0.0
    else:
        tj, tc, tcr = (L / (2 * j)) ** (1 / 3), 0.0, 0.0
    durations = [tj, tc, tj, tcr, tj, tc, tj]
    jerks = [j, 0, -j, 0, -j, 0, j]
    T = sum(durations)
    t = np.linspace(0, T, int(math.ceil(T / dt)) + 1)
    s_, v_, a_, j_ = np.zeros_like(t), np.zeros_like(t), np.zeros_like(t), np.zeros_like(t)
    t0 = s0 = v0 = a0 = 0.0
    for d, jj in zip(durations, jerks):
        m = (t >= t0 - 1e-12) & (t <= t0 + d + 1e-9)
        ta = t[m] - t0
        j_[m] = jj
        a_[m] = a0 + jj * ta
        v_[m] = v0 + a0 * ta + jj * ta ** 2 / 2
        s_[m] = s0 + v0 * ta + a0 * ta ** 2 / 2 + jj * ta ** 3 / 6
        s0 += v0 * d + a0 * d ** 2 / 2 + jj * d ** 3 / 6
        v0 += a0 * d + jj * d ** 2 / 2
        a0 += jj * d
        t0 += d
    return dict(t=t, s=np.clip(s_, 0, L), v=np.maximum(v_, 0), a=a_, jt=j_, T=T, s_end=s0, v_end=v0)


def time_profile(traj):
    pr = sample_phases(traj["plan"]["phases"], traj["L"]) if traj.get("slowed_pairs") else s_curve_profile(traj["L"])
    s = pr["s"]
    pr["x"] = np.interp(s, traj["S"], traj["XY"][:, 0])
    pr["y"] = np.interp(s, traj["S"], traj["XY"][:, 1])
    pr["kappa"] = np.interp(s, traj["S"], traj["K"])
    pr["an"] = pr["v"] ** 2 * pr["kappa"]
    dxy = np.gradient(traj["XY"], axis=0)
    pr["heading"] = np.interp(s, traj["S"], np.unwrap(np.arctan2(dxy[:, 1], dxy[:, 0])))
    return pr


def make_scenario(seed):
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    while True:
        m = random_map(rng)
        if m is None:
            continue
        A, B, obstacles = m
        zones = safety_zones(obstacles)
        if not straight_line_blocked(A, B, zones):
            continue
        overlapping = []
        traj = find_trajectory(A, B, zones, overlapping)
        if traj is None or not traj["seq"]:
            continue
        max_angle = max([math.degrees(t["Delta"]) for t in traj["turns"] if t["R"] < t["rho"] - 1e-6] or [0])
        if max_angle >= MIN_TURN_ANGLE:
            break
    traj = check_touching_turns(A, B, zones, traj, overlapping)
    return assemble_scenario(seed, A, B, obstacles, traj)


def assemble_scenario(seed, A, B, obstacles, traj):
    pr = time_profile(traj)
    t_at = lambda s_: float(np.interp(s_, pr["s"], pr["t"]))
    marks = [(0.0, "A", "start, v = 0")]
    if traj.get("slowed_pairs"):
        marks += [(t_, name, desc) for t_, s_, name, desc in traj["plan"]["marks"]]
        marks += [(t_at(c["s_X"]), "X", f"turns touch, v_s = {c['v_s']:.2f} m/s") for c in traj["slowed_pairs"]]
    else:
        marks += [(t_at(S_ACC), "P1", f"reached v_max {V} m/s"), (t_at(traj["L"] - S_ACC), "P2", "braking starts")]
    marks += [(t_at(s_), name, desc) for s_, name, desc in traj["geom_marks"]]
    marks.append((pr["T"], "B", "arrived, v = 0"))
    marks.sort(key=lambda z: z[0])
    marks = [(tm, name, desc, np.array([np.interp(tm, pr["t"], pr["x"]), np.interp(tm, pr["t"], pr["y"])]))
             for tm, name, desc in marks]
    return dict(seed=seed, A=A, B=B, obstacles=obstacles, zones=safety_zones(obstacles), traj=traj, profile=pr, marks=marks)


def s_curve_case(dx, d, R=6.0, A=(10, 90), B=(90, 10), x1=40):
    obstacles = [(np.array([x1, 50 + d + 12 * k], float), R) for k in range(6)] + \
                [(np.array([x1 + dx, 50 - d - 12 * k], float), R) for k in range(6)]
    A, B = np.array(A, float), np.array(B, float)
    zones = safety_zones(obstacles)
    overlapping = []
    traj = check_touching_turns(A, B, zones, find_trajectory(A, B, zones, overlapping), overlapping)
    return assemble_scenario(f"S-curve dx={dx:g} d={d:g}", A, B, obstacles, traj)

    def step(self, _):

    Conclusion

    Summary

    High-school geometry and kinematics are enough to plan fast flights that remain physically valid. Paths built from straight segments, clothoids and arcs, combined with a jerk-limited speed profile, reach every limit of the drone without exceeding it. On the 289 benchmark maps it solved, the planned time is a median of 0.014 s above a theoretical lower bound.

    Limitations

    The planner slows down only where two turns would otherwise overlap, whereas modern methods brake and accelerate wherever it saves time. It works in a plane, and it treats the drone as a point following a path rather than a vehicle with motors, inertia and air resistance. Section 7 describes these issues with an example.

    Topics for further study

    Minimum-snap trajectories
    Smooth polynomial paths for quadrotors through a sequence of waypoints.
    Time-optimal waypoint flight
    Planning with the full quadrotor dynamics instead of a point model.
    Model predictive control
    Re-planning many times per second to correct for wind and tracking errors.
    Reinforcement learning for racing
    Control policies learned in simulation that have beaten human champion pilots.

    I hope to study the newest algorithms in this field in more depth, starting with these four topics.
